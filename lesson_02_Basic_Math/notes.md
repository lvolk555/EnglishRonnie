## 1. Core Vocabulary

- **math / mathematics** — the study of numbers, shapes, and how to count and measure. (数学)
- **arithmetic** — an old word for basic math (adding, subtracting, multiplying, dividing). It is very old-fashioned — we don't use it today. (算术，已过时的词)
- **symbol / sign** — a small mark that means something, like +, −, ÷, ×. (符号)
- **equation** — a math sentence with numbers, like 5 + 7 = 12. (等式)
- **answer** — the result of a math problem. (答案)
- **equals** — the word we use for the "=" sign. (等于)

---

## 2. The 4 Basic Operations — What We REALLY Say

This is the most important part of the lesson:

| Symbol | Formal word | Spoken word | Example | Say it like |
|--------|-------------|-------------|---------|-------------|
| + | **add** (加) | **plus** | 5 + 7 | five **plus** seven |
| − | **subtract** (减) | **minus** | 7 − 1 | seven **minus** one |
| ÷ | **divide** (除) | **divided by** | 42 ÷ 2 | forty-two **divided by** two |
| × | **multiply** (乘) | **times** | 9 × 7 | nine **times** seven |

**Rule:** In everyday English, when we say an equation OUT LOUD, we do NOT say "add / subtract / divide / multiply". We say **plus / minus / divided by / times**. The formal words are used in writing or formal situations.

Examples from the lesson:
- 5 + 7 = 12 → "Five **plus** seven **equals** twelve."
- 7 − 1 = ? → "Seven **minus** one equals..." (homework!)
- 42 ÷ 2 = 21 → "Forty-two **divided by** two equals twenty-one."
- 9 × 7 = 63 → "Nine **times** seven equals sixty-three."

---

## 3. Grammar & Usage Notes

1. **equals** — always singular, even with big numbers:
   - ✅ Five plus seven **equals** twelve.
   - ❌ Five plus seven **equal** twelve.
2. **times** — even though the × symbol looks like the letter "X", we say "times", NOT "X".
   - ❌ nine X seven
   - ✅ nine **times** seven
3. **divided by** — we say it with a short pause: "42 **divided by** 2".

## 4. Ronnie's Magic Trick: The 9 Times Table (手指速算法)

How to multiply any number from 1 to 10 by 9, using your fingers:

1. Hold up both hands (10 fingers).
2. Count from left to right until you reach the number you want × 9.
3. Put that finger DOWN.
4. Fingers on the LEFT = the tens digit (十位数).
   Fingers on the RIGHT = the ones digit (个位数).

Examples:
- **9 × 7**: put the 7th finger down → left: 6, right: 3 → **63**
- **9 × 9**: put the 9th finger down → left: 8, right: 1 → **81**

It only works up to 10 — after that you run out of fingers!

## 5. Useful Phrases from the Lesson

- **to figure out** — to find the answer by thinking. "I can't figure out this math problem." (算出来/弄明白)
- **to run out of** — to have no more of something. "I've run out of fingers." (用完/耗尽)
- **math terrifies me** — math scares me very much. (数学吓死我了)
- **to get an answer right** — to answer correctly. "I got an answer right!" (答对了)
- **It doesn't matter.** — It is not important. (没关系/无所谓)
