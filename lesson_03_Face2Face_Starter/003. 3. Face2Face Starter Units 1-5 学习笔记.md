## 一、你的学习情况分析

### 1. 进度换算

| 指标 | 数据 | 解读 |
|---|---|---|
| 学习时长 | 1 小时 | 首次接触这本教材 |
| 覆盖页数 | 40+ 页 | 从 p6 开始 = Unit 1–5 全部（每单元恰好 8 页） |
| 平均速度 | ≈1.4 分钟/页 | A1 零基础阶段**偏快** |
| 已过语法 | be 动词（人称/单复数/否定/疑问）、人称与物主代词、名词复数、a/an、指示代词、一般现在时、时间介词、频率副词 | A1 语法主干已全部露过一面 |
| 已过词汇 | 数字 0–100、国家/国籍、职业、家庭、饮食、日常活动、衣物、星期、时间表达 | A1 核心词汇约 250–300 个 |

### 2. 三个风险（按严重程度排序）

**风险 1：假性熟练（最常见）**——"看着句子觉得都懂"。A1 内容简单，容易产生"我已经会了"的错觉。检验方法：遮住英文说中文、遮住中文说英文，说不出来 = 没会。

**风险 2：跳过音频**——每个单元都有 *Help with Listening*（Unit 1 练单词重音、Unit 2 练数字 -teen/-ty、Unit 3 练缩读、Unit 4 练数字听辨）+ Real World 听力。A1 阶段听力输入的重要性高于读写，1 小时刷 5 个单元几乎不可能认真听完所有音频。

**风险 3：没有输出**——只读不说。按艾宾浩斯遗忘曲线，纯阅读输入 1 天后留存约 33%，1 周后约 20%；而"读 + 跟读 + 造句 + 自测"可以把 1 周留存拉到 60–80%。

### 3. 建议的学习闭环（每单元 2–2.5 小时，比 1 小时刷 5 个单元效果好 3 倍以上）

```
第 1 步 听（20 min）  合上书听本单元音频，能听出大意再打开书
第 2 步 读（20 min）  打开书朗读词汇和句型，模仿语音语调（录音对比）
第 3 步 练（40 min）  做课本练习 + Workbook 对应页（Unit 1→p52 ... Unit 5→p60）
第 4 步 说（30 min）  Real World 功能句背熟；用每单元句型造 5 个自己的句子
第 5 步 测（20 min）  遮住中文说英文 / 听写 10 个单词 / 做 Extra Practice
复习节点：当天晚上 10 分钟 → 第 2 天 10 分钟 → 第 7 天 15 分钟 → 第 30 天 15 分钟
```

## 二、分单元主题笔记

### Unit 1（p6–13）People & Introductions 人物与介绍

#### 1A What's your name?（p6）
- **词汇主题**：numbers 0–12（zero … twelve）；hello / hi / goodbye / bye
- **语法**：I, my, you, your（"I'm Tom." "My name's Tom."）
- **核心句型**：
  - What's your name? — My name's … / I'm …
  - Nice to meet you. — Nice to meet you too.
  - How are you? — I'm fine, thanks. And you?
- **Real World**：saying hello; introducing people（"This is Maria."）；phone numbers（读法：oh 代替零，double 代替重复，如 0550 = "oh five five oh"）

#### 1B Where's she from?（p8）
- **词汇主题**：countries（England, Britain, the UK, the US, America, China, Japan, France, Italy, Spain, Brazil, Australia）
- **语法**：he / his / she / her
- **核心句型**：Where are you from? — I'm from China. / Where's she from? — She's from Japan.
- ⚠️ **易错**：问句中用 **Where are you from?**（不是 Where do you from?）；国家前不加介词 in，from 已含"来自"之意。
- **Help with Listening**：单词重音（CHI-na, JA-pan）

#### 1C In class（p10）
- **词汇主题**：the alphabet（字母表）；things in your bag (1)（ID card, pen, pencil, book, notebook, phone, keys, umbrella）；a and an
- **语法**：first names and surnames（名前姓后）；classroom language（课堂用语）
- **核心句型**：
  - a + 辅音音素：a book, a pen, a university /j/、a European /j/
  - an + 元音音素：an apple, an umbrella, an hour（h 不发音）、an MP3 player
- **课堂用语**：Open your book. / Listen and repeat. / Read the sentence. / Work in pairs. / Close your book.

#### 1D People and things（p12）
- **词汇主题**：people（man, woman, boy, girl, baby, friend）；things（table, chair, watch, phone, key, umbrella, bag）；plurals
- **语法**：名词复数规则
  - 一般 +s：book → books, phone → phones
  - s/x/sh/ch 结尾 +es：watch → watches, box → boxes
  - 辅音 + y → ies：family → families, city → cities
  - 不规则：man → men, woman → women, child → children, person → people
- **核心句型**：What's this? — It's a watch. / What are these? — They're keys.（见插图 1）

### Unit 2（p14–21）Personal Information 个人信息

#### 2A She's British（p14）
- **词汇主题**：nationalities（British, American, Chinese, Japanese, French, Italian, Spanish, Australian）
- **语法**：be (singular) 肯定与否定：I'm not …, He isn't …, She isn't …
- ⚠️ **易错**：国籍首字母**永远大写**（Chinese ≠ chinese）；be 动词不随"多个形容词"变化。
- **Real World**：true or false（听后判断正误："She's Italian." — True or false?）

#### 2B What's your job?（p16）
- **词汇主题**：jobs（teacher, student, doctor, nurse, manager, engineer, waiter, shop assistant, receptionist, police officer）
- **语法**：be (singular) 疑问句与简短回答：Is he a doctor? — Yes, he is. / No, he isn't.
- ⚠️ **易错**：职业前用 **a/an**（He's *a* teacher），但 "What's your job?" 本身不加冠词。

#### 2C Personal information（p18）
- **词汇主题**：titles（Mr, Mrs, Ms, Miss）；greetings（good morning/afternoon/evening/night）
- **语法**：email addresses 读法（@ = "at"，. = "dot"）；personal information questions
- **Real World**：filling in a form（填表：first name / surname / age / nationality / email）
- ⚠️ **Mr/Mrs/Ms/Miss 后接姓氏**：Mr Smith ✔，Mr John ✘

#### 2D How old is she?（p20）
- **词汇主题**：numbers 13–100（thirteen … twenty … ninety … one hundred）
- **语法**：How old …? 问年龄
- ⚠️ **发音陷阱 -teen vs -ty**：
  - **-teen** [tiːn] 重音在词尾：thir**teen** [ˌθɜːˈtiːn], fif**teen**, eigh**teen**
  - **-ty** [ti] 重音在词首：**thir**ty [ˈθɜːti], **fif**ty, **eigh**ty
  - 听力中 thirteen / thirty 分不清是 A1 最大丢分点，跟读时刻意拖长 -teen 的尾音
- **Help with Listening**：数字辨音（-teen and -ty）

### Unit 3（p22–29）Cities, Family & Food 城市、家庭与饮食

#### 3A Two cities（p22）
- **词汇主题**：adjectives (1)（big, small, old, new, young, beautiful, expensive, cheap, busy, noisy, quiet, famous）；very
- **语法**：be (plural)：We're …, They aren't …, Are they …? — Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.
- **语序**：形容词放 be 动词后 / 名词前，多个形容词顺序：大小 → 新旧 → 颜色（a big new red car）
- **Help with Listening**：contractions（缩读：I'm, you're, he's, she's, isn't, aren't）

#### 3B Brothers and sisters（p24）
- **词汇主题**：family（mother, father, parents, brother, sister, son, daughter, child, children, grandmother, grandfather, wife, husband）
- **语法**：possessive 's（Tom's bag, my mother's car）；subject pronouns + possessive adjectives
- **代词全家福**：
  | 主格 | 形容词性物主代词 | 名词性物主代词 |
  |---|---|---|
  | I | my | mine |
  | you | your | yours |
  | he | his | his |
  | she | her | hers |
  | we | our | ours |
  | they | their | theirs |

#### 3C Eat in or take away?（p26）
- **词汇主题**：food and drink (1)（coffee, tea, water, milk, juice, sandwich, hamburger, pizza, salad, apple, banana, egg, bread, cake, ice cream）；money and prices（pound, dollar, euro）
- **语法**：How much …? 问价格：How much is it? — It's £5.（英镑符号 £ 读 pound）
- **Real World**：ordering food and drink in a café（点餐三句：Can I have a coffee, please? / How much is that? / Here you are.）
- ⚠️ **不可数名词无复数**：water ✘waters, bread ✘breads（要说 two glasses of water / two pieces of bread）

#### 3D Food I like（p28）
- **词汇主题**：food and drink (2)（rice, fish, chicken, cheese, vegetables, fruit, chocolate, cola）；love, like, eat, drink, a lot of
- **语法**：love / like + 名词；eat / drink + 名词；a lot of + 名词（"I like Italian food a lot."）
- ⚠️ **like 后接动词要加 -ing**：I like swimming ✔（I like swim ✘）

### Unit 4（p30–37）Daily Life 日常生活

#### 4A Live, work and study（p30）
- **词汇主题**：phrases with like, have, live, work, study（like coffee, have a car, live in a flat, work in an office, study English）
- **语法**：Present Simple (I, you, we, they) 肯定/否定：I work … / I don't work …
- ⚠️ **三单预告**：本单元只学 I/you/we/they，Unit 5A 立刻转 he/she/it，是全书第一大语法台阶，务必在本单元把原形动词读熟。

#### 4B My free time（p32）
- **词汇主题**：free time activities（watch TV, watch films, read books, listen to music, play football/tennis/the guitar, go shopping, go swimming, go to the cinema, go out with friends）
- **语法**：Present Simple 疑问与简短回答：Do you …? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
- ⚠️ **介词不能丢**：listen **to** music ✔，play **the** guitar/piano ✔（球类不加 the：play football ✔）
- **Real World**：an online interview（谈论爱好）

#### 4C Buying things（p34）
- **词汇主题**：things to buy（T-shirt, shirt, trousers, jeans, dress, skirt, shoes, jacket, coat, sweater, hat, bag, watch）；this, that, these, those
- **语法**：指示代词（见 Unit 1D 插图）；buy / sell / want / need
- **核心句型**：How much is this T-shirt? / How much are these shoes?（单数用 is，复数用 are）
- **Real World**：buying things in a shop（购物对话：Can I help you? / I'd like … / How much is it? / I'll take it.）

#### 4D What time is it?（p36）
- **词汇主题**：days of the week（Monday … Sunday，**首字母永远大写**）；time words（o'clock, half past, quarter past, quarter to, morning, afternoon, evening, night）
- **语法**：telling the time：It's seven o'clock. / It's half past seven.（7:30）/ It's quarter past seven.（7:15）/ It's quarter to eight.（7:45）
- ⚠️ **时间介词**：at + 钟点，on + 星期/日期，in + 时段/月份/年份（见插图 2）

### Unit 5（p38–45）Routines 日常规律

#### 5A A typical day（p38）
- **词汇主题**：daily routines（get up, have a shower, have breakfast, go to work, go to school, start work, have lunch, finish work, go home, get home, have dinner, watch TV, go to bed）
- **语法**：Present Simple (he, she, it) 三单变化——**全书第一大语法台阶**：
  - 一般 +s：work → works, live → lives
  - s/x/ch/sh/o 结尾 +es：watch → watches, go → goes, do → does
  - 辅音 + y → ies：study → studies
  - 特殊：have → has
  - 否定：doesn't + 原形（He doesn't **work** — 注意动词回原形）
  - 疑问：Does he work …? — Yes, he does. / No, he doesn't.

#### 5B Where does she work?（p40）
- **词汇主题**：time phrases with on, at, in（on Monday, at 7 o'clock, in the morning）
- **语法**：Does …? 问句 + 地点表达（work in a hospital / at home / in an office）
- ⚠️ **at the weekend**（英式）= on the weekend（美式）

#### 5C An evening out（p42）
- **词汇主题**：food and drink (3) / restaurant language（menu, bill, waiter, waitress, mineral water, a glass of wine, table for two）
- **语法**：复习 would like（I'd like …）与点餐句型
- **Real World**：ordering in a restaurant（全套点餐流程）

#### 5D A day off（p44）
- **词汇主题**：frequency adverbs and phrases with every（always, usually, often, sometimes, never; every day, every morning, every week, every weekend, every year）
- **语法**：频率副词位置——**实义动词前**（I always get up at 7.），**be 动词后**（He is always late.）
- ⚠️ **never 本身含否定**：He never works ✘ doesn't never；usually 重音在首音节 [ˈjuːʒuəli]

## 四、语法速查表

### 1. be 动词全变位

| 人称 | 肯定 | 否定 | 疑问 | 简短回答 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | I **am** (I'm) | I **am not** | **Am** I …? | Yes, I am. / No, I'm not. |
| you / we / they | you **are** (you're) | you **aren't** | **Are** you …? | Yes, we are. / No, we aren't. |
| he / she / it | he **is** (he's) | he **isn't** | **Is** he …? | Yes, he is. / No, he isn't. |

### 2. 一般现在时（Present Simple）

| | 肯定 | 否定 | 疑问 |
|---|---|---|---|
| I / you / we / they | I work. | I **don't** work. | **Do** you work? |
| he / she / it | He **works**. | He **doesn't work**（动词回原形） | **Does** he work? |

三单变化口诀：**一般加 s，s/x/ch/sh/o 加 es，辅音+y 变 ies，have 变 has**。

### 3. a / an 的选择
看**发音**不看字母：a book / a university [j] / a European [j] ｜ an apple / an umbrella / an hour（h 不发音）/ an MP3 [e]

### 4. 名词复数
一般 +s ｜ s/x/sh/ch +es ｜ 辅音+y→ies ｜ f/fe→ves（wife→wives）｜ 不规则：man→men, woman→women, child→children, person→people

### 5. 时间介词
**at** + 钟点（at 7 o'clock）｜ **on** + 星期/日期（on Monday, on 17 May）｜ **in** + 时段/月/年（in the morning, in May, in 2024）

### 6. 频率副词位置
实义动词**前**：I **always** get up at 7. ｜ be 动词**后**：He is **always** late.

## 五、发音重点（Help with Pronunciation）

| 单元 | 对立音 | 例词 | 跟读要点 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unit 1 | /æ/ vs /ə/ | **man** [mæn] vs a**bout** [əˈbaʊt] | /æ/ 嘴巴张大扁唇；/ə/ 最轻最短 |
| Unit 2 | /ɪ/ vs /iː/ | s**i**t [sɪt] vs s**ee**d [siːd]；sh**i**p vs sh**ee**p | /iː/ 嘴角向两边拉长，肌肉紧 |
| Unit 3 | /ɒ/ vs /ə/ | h**o**t [hɒt] vs a**bout** [əˈbaʊt] | /ɒ/ 圆唇张大（英音）；美音近似 /ɑː/ |
| Unit 4 | /v/ vs /ð/ | **v**ery [ˈveri] vs **th**e [ðə] | /v/ 上齿咬下唇；/ð/ 舌尖轻触上齿送气 |

另加两个 A1 顽固音：
- **-teen [tiːn] vs -ty [ti]**：thir**teen** vs **thir**ty（重音位置相反）
- **work / world / word** 中的 or 在英音中读 [ɜː]（[wɜːk]），不要读成"沃克"
