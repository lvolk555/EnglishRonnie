# ✅ 答案页

**1.** 1. drinks  2. love  3. live  4. likes  5. teaches

**2.** 1. I'm not from the USA.  2. My parents don't live in a flat.

**3.** 1. studies  2. goes  3. watches  4. has  5. plays  6. misses

**4.** 1. in  2. on  3. on  4. at  5. at  6. on

**5.** 1. seven o'clock  2. half past eight  3. (a) quarter past nine  4. (a) quarter to eleven

**6.** 1. Does Luke go to bed at ten thirty?  2. She gets up early every day.

**7.** 1. There is (There's)  2. There aren't  3. There are  4. There isn't  5. There are

**8.** 1. There's **a** post office.（单数用 a）  2. any  3. a  4. some  5. a

**9.** 1. Is there a park in Greenwich?  2. Are there any good museums?  3. Are there any restaurants near Henry's flat?  4. Is there a supermarket near the station?

**10.** 1. opposite  2. next to  3. near  4. on your right, next to

**11.** 1. but Ian hates **it**  2. Do you know **him**? — ...but I don't like **him**  3. She plays tennis with **her**.

**12.** 1. he  2. I / him  3. We / them  4. She / me  5. He / us

**13.** 1. loves watching（likes watching 亦可）  2. love dancing（like dancing 亦可）  3. don't mind flying  4. hates getting up

**14.** 1. was  2. was  3. were  4. were  5. Was  6. were（people 是复数）

**15.** 1. Where **were** you last night?  2. I **was** born in 2001.

**16.** 1. (the) fifth of May, nineteen ninety-eight / May (the) fifth, nineteen ninety-eight  2. (the) twenty-third of April, twenty thirteen

**17.** 1. went  2. had  3. stayed  4. took  5. bought  6. travelled  7. got  8. left

**18.** 1. went  2. stayed  3. didn't come  4. Did / buy / did

**19.** 1. Where did you go last year? (+) Yes, I did.（按题意可写简答；若问地点，答具体地点亦可）  2. Did they stay in a hotel? (−) No, they didn't.

**20.** 1. 'm going to study (am going to study)  2. isn't going to get up  3. Are / going to move  4. are / going to do

**21.** 1. Yes, I am.  2. No, he isn't.

**22.** 1. My brother **has** three cars.  2. Does she **work** here?  3. **Is** there a bank near here?  4. She loves **him**.  5. I didn't **go** to school yesterday.

**23.** 1. He gets up at seven o'clock every day.  2. There's a museum and some cafés in the town. / There are some cafés and a museum in the town.  3. I went to Guilin last year and stayed (for) four days.  4. I'm going to look for a job next year.  5. Can you speak Chinese?
